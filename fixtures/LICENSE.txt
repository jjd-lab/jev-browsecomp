The markdown files in fixtures/corpus, the questions in fixtures/gold.jsonl
other than the six abstain rows, and the HotpotQA answer strings recorded in
fixtures/provenance.jsonl are a small slice of HotpotQA dev distractor v1.

HotpotQA: Zhilin Yang, Peng Qi, Saizheng Zhang, Yoshua Bengio, William W. Cohen,
Ruslan Salakhutdinov, and Christopher D. Manning. HotpotQA: A Dataset for
Diverse, Explainable Multi-hop Question Answering. EMNLP 2018.
https://hotpotqa.github.io/

HotpotQA is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Passage text is Wikipedia content as frozen in that HotpotQA dump, credited to
Wikipedia contributors, also under CC BY-SA 4.0. Article titles are in
fixtures/provenance.jsonl. Those titles identify the Wikipedia articles. The
sentences can differ from the live article: HotpotQA does not ship a revision
id. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Text_of_the_Creative_Commons_Attribution-ShareAlike_4.0_International_License

Changes made here:
- Each supporting article is one markdown file. The title is the only section heading.
- Leading and trailing whitespace on each sentence was removed.
- Sentences containing a double quote, a newline, or the characters {' were omitted so a cited span stays one mechanical quote.
- A birth-date stub sentence shorter than 40 characters and containing "(bef." was omitted.
- Node ids are assigned by this repo from the title. Kept sentence wording was not rewritten.
- Only the two supporting articles for each selected question are included. The eight HotpotQA distractor paragraphs per question are not included.
- Bridge and easy gold ids are the one supporting sentence that contains the HotpotQA answer string. Multi gold ids are both supporting sentences.
- Six abstain questions were written for this repo. They are not HotpotQA items. They are not Wikipedia text.
